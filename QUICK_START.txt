🆘 TROUBLESHOOTING:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Problem: PDF not generating
Fix: chmod -R 775 storage && php artisan storage:link

Problem: Email not sending
Fix: Check .env email config, test SMTP connection

Problem: Form not loading
Fix: php artisan route:clear && php artisan config:clear

Problem: 500 error
Fix: Check storage/logs/laravel.log
